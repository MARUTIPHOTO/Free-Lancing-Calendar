Privacy Policy

Application: FreeLancing Calendar (Developer: Maruti Photo & Films)

1. Introduction & Scope

Welcome to FreeLancing Calendar ("we", "our", or "us"), provided and operated by Maruti Photo & Films. FreeLancing Calendar is a mobile application tailored specifically for freelance photographers, cinematographers, drone pilots, and studio creators to organize event dates, manage client inquiries, calculate package rates, track booking statuses, and securely synchronize scheduling data.

Your privacy is of paramount importance to us. This Privacy Policy informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, disclosure, and safeguarding of personal data when you use our mobile application and related services, and the choices you have associated with that data.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to provide our calendar scheduling, calculation, and booking management features:

👤 Account & Authentication Data

When you sign in using Google Sign-In (powered by Google Credential Manager and Firebase Authentication), we receive basic profile details including your Name and Email Address. This establishes your unique identity to isolate and protect your bookings.

📋 Booking & Business Data

Information you manually input to manage your gigs, including: client name, contact phone number, event dates, event types (Wedding, Pre-Wedding, Reception, etc.), customized rates, equipment/camera details, and inquiry notes.

📱 Device & Network Telemetry

To ensure reliable cloud sync and prevent data loss, the app checks network connectivity state (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, INTERNET). We do not collect continuous device location, IMEI numbers, or hardware identifiers.

🔔 Push Notifications

With your permission (POST_NOTIFICATIONS on Android 13+), we deliver local and cloud alerts when new client inquiries arrive or upcoming event dates approach.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Calendar & Booking Management: To organize, display, filter, and track booked dates, confirmed dates, and pending inquiries.
  • Cross-Device Cloud Synchronization: To sync your bookings seamlessly between your devices through Google Firebase Cloud Firestore so you never lose your records if you reinstall the app.
  • Quotation Generation: To calculate transparent rates based on your custom photography and videography rate cards and generate WhatsApp-ready sharing summaries.
  • Direct Freelance Communication: To permit you to initiate WhatsApp or phone calls to clients directly from your verified booking entries.
  • Service Reliability & App Performance: To detect app crashes and ensure proper functioning of offline SQLite cache and cloud database sync.

4. Data Storage & Security

We employ industry-standard technical safeguards to protect your personal and business records:

🔒 Local Device Storage: All event entries are immediately stored on your device in a sandboxed, encrypted Room SQLite database, allowing full offline usage even in remote wedding shooting venues with no cellular coverage.

☁️ Secure Cloud Database: Cloud synchronization is handled by Google Firebase Firestore located in secure Google Cloud data centers. Access is strictly controlled through zero-trust Firestore Security Rules requiring authenticated Google credentials.

🛡️ In-Transit Encryption: All data transmitted between your device and Google servers is encrypted using modern TLS / HTTPS protocols.

We adhere strictly to Google Play's User Data policy and data minimization principles:

In-App Deletion: You can edit or delete individual booking entries, dates, or rate card configurations anytime directly within the app's interface.

Account & Cloud Data Deletion Request: You have the right to permanently delete your account, authentication records, and all synchronized cloud booking data. You can initiate a complete deletion request by emailing:

7. Children's Privacy

FreeLancing Calendar is a professional freelance business and scheduling utility intended solely for working professionals aged 18 and older (or at least the minimum age required to contract in your jurisdiction). The application is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we immediately delete it from our servers.

8. Android Permissions Disclosure

In compliance with Google Play Store standards, the specific purposes for each requested permission are described below:

Permission Declared Purpose
INTERNET Allows communication with Google Firebase backend to sync user bookings across devices and process Google Sign-In.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Allows the app to check whether an active Wi-Fi or cellular network is available before attempting cloud sync.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Enables system notifications for upcoming photography shoots, client inquiries, and schedule updates.
